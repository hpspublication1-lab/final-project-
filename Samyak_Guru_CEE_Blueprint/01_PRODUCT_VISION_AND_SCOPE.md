# Product Vision, Scope and Feature Catalog

## Product
Samyak Guru is a multi-program learning platform. CEE Visual Intelligence is a **separately scoped** visual science-learning subsystem for Nepal's medical entrance examination preparation. Other programs (SEE, NEB, IOE, IELTS, Lokseva) must not bleed into CEE.

## Student journey
Program selection → verified syllabus → subject → chapter → topic → lesson → learning objectives → educator explanation → interactive visual → guided animation → visual practice → approved CEE MCQs → feedback → mastery/progress → revision.

## Visual catalog — syllabus verification mandatory
**Biology:** Human cardiovascular (heart, chambers, valves, pulmonary/systemic circulation, cardiac cycle), respiratory (alveoli, diaphragm, gas exchange), digestive (organs, enzymes, villi), renal (nephron, filtration, reabsorption, ADH), nervous (neuron, synapse, reflex arc), endocrine, reproductive, skeletal/muscular, sensory, immunity, blood, pathogens; cells and organelles, membrane transport, mitosis/meiosis, DNA replication/transcription/translation, Mendelian inheritance, pedigree, respiration; plant photosynthesis, xylem/phloem, roots/stems/leaves, reproduction, double fertilization; zoology, animal classification, ecology, life cycles.

**Chemistry:** atomic orbitals (avoid misleading planetary depiction), bonding, molecular structures, isomerism, organic mechanisms, kinetics, thermodynamics, equilibrium, acids/bases, electrochemistry, titration and analytical labs.

**Physics:** mechanics, pendulum, projectiles, energy, electricity/circuits, fields, magnetism, optics, waves, thermodynamics, photoelectric effect, semiconductors and nuclear topics. Simulations show equations, units, variable ranges and idealizations.

**Mental Agility:** pattern recognition, numerical reasoning, spatial rotations, logic and visual sequences. Practice interactions distinct from official exam-style single-best-answer MCQs.

Every item must be checked against the **specific official syllabus version and program** before publishing; do not hardcode assumed exam topic weights.

## Student-facing feature set
Universal player with rotate/zoom/pan, layer isolation, clipping, explode views where useful, hotspots/labels, timeline/speed, guided tours, side-by-side comparisons, accessible descriptions, reduced motion, captions, fallback diagrams, progress and offline support as phased capability.

## Foundation deliverables
Curriculum registry, program isolation, asset upload/processing, visual player, lesson revision CMS, academic review and publication, role-based access, audit logs, staging and tests. AI tutor, adaptive mastery, large content library and offline packs are **future phases**, not Foundation release blockers unless existing infrastructure makes them trivial.

## Constraints
No fabricated lessons, demo data in production, false claims of Cambridge/MEC endorsement, or unsupported claims of official scoring. Use original/licensed assets. Preserve preexisting resources.
